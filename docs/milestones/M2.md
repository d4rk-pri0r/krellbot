**Exit:** a stranger can install from the site path, open the UI via bare `krellbot` / `ui`, enable the OS scheduler from the app and see status, arm a paper bot on **BTC/USD**, and have ticks capable of evaluating entries (not forever `metadata_refusal` for lack of rules). `soak_report.py` cannot `PASS` with zero evaluated entry decisions; `metadata_refusal` raises an alert. `PAPER_ONLY_RELEASE` stays `True`.

Rules for every task are in `AGENTS.md`. Agents skip `[owner]` tasks. Agents pick the lowest-numbered unchecked engineering task that has no open PR and does not depend on an unmerged task.

**Parallelism:** M2.1 ∥ M2.4 ∥ M2.6 ∥ M2.7 ∥ M2.9; M2.3 owner ∥ all; M2.5 after or with M2.4; M2.8 after M2.7; M2.2 after M2.1 (and green only after M2.3 for the full site path).

**Revises owner soak:** After M2.4–M2.6 + M2.7–M2.8, **M1.12** is runnable as a meaningful seven-day BTC/USD paper soak rather than hourly coverage of a bot that cannot enter.
