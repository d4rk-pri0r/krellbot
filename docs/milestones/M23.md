# M23 / P4: distribution

**Phase:** P4 — Distribution.

**Exit / gate:** **100 paying customers** and a **second revenue line
> $1k/mo** (referral fee-share and/or creator pack rev share), with
referral attribution, signed creator pack format + rev share, a
**second venue** in the product sense, and a **headless runner** for
always-on paper/live ticks without inventing CLI user features.
Unfinished M0–M22 work stays in those files. Fake transports in CI.
Do not invent a Learn view.

**Why this order:** monetization beyond one pack family needs
attribution and creators; venue breadth for live/distribution follows
paper-first M14 work without forcing multi-venue live in P1.

Rules for every task are in `AGENTS.md`. Agents skip `[owner]` tasks.
**Do not start M23 engineering until the owner merges the roadmap PR
and P3 gate is accepted (or owner explicitly waives sequencing).**

**Parallelism:** M23.1 ∥ M23.2; M23.3 after M23.2; M23.4 after M14
paper breadth as needed; M23.5 ∥ M23.4; M23.6 [owner] gate last.
