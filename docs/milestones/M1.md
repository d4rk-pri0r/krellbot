# M1: the live path is proven on Kraken, one flag from real orders

**Exit:** every task below is checked, with `PAPER_ONLY_RELEASE` still `True`. What remains before a real order is one owner decision (gate G1 at the bottom), not engineering.

**Scope:** Kraken spot, BTC/USD, one armed pack, the web workstation. Coinbase live, multiple packs per market, and the pack store are out of scope.

**How tests reach live code:** through a pytest fixture that monkeypatches `release_policy.PAPER_ONLY_RELEASE` and injects a fake Kraken transport. No environment variable, config file, or build flag may enable live sends, and nothing in a frozen build may be able to reach the fake venue.

**What exists at drafting (2026-11-11):** ADR 0001 routes live placements through the Outbox, `entries_paused` persists in config, tick blocks entries while a reconcile is outstanding, and backup/restore exists. Issue #371 says the four safety obligations were not re-proven on `main`. M1.1 turns that into four tests so the answer is pass or fail, not an audit.

Rules for every task are in `AGENTS.md`. Every task here except M1.9 and M1.10 touches a risk path.

## G1: the first real order (owner gate, not an agent task)

Kraken spot has no sandbox. `validate=true` checks a request's inputs and never submits it, so M1 cannot observe a real acknowledgement, fill, partial fill, or stop trigger. M1.6 pins the adapter to what Kraken documents, which is not the same as what Kraken does.

Before 1.0, someone places one minimum-size BTC/USD entry with a protective stop through the workstation on a real account and confirms the journal reconciles to Kraken's own trade history. Decide who that is before M2 starts. Until G1 passes, `PAPER_ONLY_RELEASE` stays `True`.
