# Market position

Krellbot sells **self-custody automation with receipts**, not a hosted
trading account. The durable advantage is that **krellbot.dev never
receives exchange keys** — a direct contrast to cloud bots whose failure
mode is key leak.

**Activity fees beat flat subscriptions** for this category: operators
pay when they trade or when they buy packs/alerts, not for idle seat
time. Pure open source alone does not monetize; the engine stays free so
trust compounds, while Pro packs, key-blind alerts, disclosed exchange
fee-share/referral, and creator revenue share fund the lab.

Strategy distribution needs a **creator audience**. Signed pack format +
rev share (Phase P4) is how third-party authors sell without Krellbot
hosting keys or inventing personalized advice. Exchanges are shipping
**free AI agent tooling**; Krellbot’s answer is a local, policy-bound
MCP/agent surface that can read, draft, validate, backtest, and
paper-arm — never live-arm or touch keys (Phase P3).

Paid ads for trading signals are restricted or hostile in major
channels; growth leans on install trust, paper evidence, referrals, and
creator packs rather than signal advertising.

Positioning test: if a competitor can copy the pitch while holding
customer keys in the cloud, the pitch is wrong. Prefer custody,
correctness, and honesty over growth shortcuts.
