### J4. Inspect the decisions behind a result
For any bar in a result, I can see why the strategy entered, exited or did nothing.
- J4.1 From a backtest result, I can open any bar and see the decision (enter, exit, hold or stay flat) with each rule's condition marked true or false.
- J4.2 The results and the bar-by-bar trace agree: every entry and exit the results count appears in the trace, and no others.
- J4.3 The plain-language explanation of a strategy matches what it actually runs: the same rules, indicators and stop.
- J4.4 A value that isn't available says so instead of showing as zero.
- J4.5 A result with 100,000 bars stays usable. It scrolls and opens bars without freezing.
