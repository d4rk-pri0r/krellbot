### J5. Arm paper trading and observe it
I put a saved strategy into paper trading and can always see what it is doing.
- J5.1 I can arm a validated strategy in paper mode with a starting balance. It shows as armed with its venue, pair, starting cash and risk cap.
- J5.2 Arming a second strategy on the same venue and pair is refused, and the message says why.
- J5.3 As paper ticks run, the status shows position, cash, resting stop and trade history, and all of them match the paper journal.
- J5.4 The status shows when it last updated. If a read fails or the data goes stale, it says so instead of showing old numbers as current.
- J5.5 On a fresh install, the built-in rehearsal walks a sample strategy through an entry, a held position and an exit on simulated data, and every screen labels it as simulated.
