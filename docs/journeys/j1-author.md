### J1. Author a strategy without an account
A new user builds and saves their own strategy using only the free app.
- J1.1 On a fresh install with no account, no exchange key and no internet, I can open Strategies, pick a starter and get an editable draft.
- J1.2 I can change the draft, for example an indicator length or the stop, and save it. It appears in my library as a new revision.
- J1.3 Saving with no changes tells me nothing changed and creates no new revision.
- J1.4 I can compare two revisions of a strategy and see exactly what changed between them.
- J1.5 After I restart the workstation, my strategies and all their revisions are still there, unchanged.
